Work sample: a service desk for a county IT team

I built this on my own to show how I'd approach a Jira Service Management engagement. "Riverbend County" is fictional, and this is not client work.

The situation

A county IT team of 12 supports about 900 employees. Requests arrive by email, phone and hallway conversation. Nobody can say how long anything takes, and audit wants proof that access requests are approved before they're granted.

Discovery questions I'd ask first

  • Which five request types make up most of your volume, and which ones cause the most complaints?
  • Which requests need an approval on record for audit, and who is allowed to approve them?
  • What does "urgent" mean here? Who decides, and what happens after hours?
  • Are you on Data Center today? Which Marketplace apps and custom fields do you rely on?
  • Do you need to stay on Government Cloud for data residency, or is commercial cloud allowed?

Access request flow

Employee submits on portal → Auto-route to hiring manager → Approved? → Assigned to Access queue → Fulfilled → Customer confirms → CSAT survey

If the request isn't approved, it closes with the reason logged, so the audit trail shows the request, the decision and who made it.

Automation rules

  • Page the on-call engineer when a P1 incident is created outside business hours.
  • Warn the assignee and team lead when an SLA is 75% used.
  • Auto-close resolved tickets after 5 business days with no reply, and send a CSAT survey.
  • Route by request type and department so nothing sits in a shared inbox.

How we'd know it worked

  • Share of requests that come in through the portal instead of email.
  • SLA attainment by request type, reviewed weekly with the IT lead.
  • Access requests with a recorded approval before fulfillment, with a target of 100%.
  • CSAT trend for the first 90 days after launch.

If they also need to migrate

  • Inventory Marketplace apps and check that each has a cloud version.
  • Clean up unused custom fields, workflows and schemes before moving them.
  • Confirm identity setup (SSO, user count) and the right cloud: Government Cloud or commercial.
  • Run a test migration with the Jira and Confluence Cloud Migration Assistants, then UAT with real users before cutover.
