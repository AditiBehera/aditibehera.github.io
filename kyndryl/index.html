How I work

Case file

Four examples, each with a measured result

Expenzing · Business Analyst & Product Executive · 2025

A slow approval chain with one hidden bottleneck

S
Invoice approvals across a 7-layer workflow were taking longer than clients expected.
T
Find where time was being lost and fix it without redesigning the whole process.
A
Mapped each approval layer, measured processing hours per transaction, isolated a 10-item batch step, and delivered the fix with IT within days of escalation.
R
Average cycle time dropped 14.6%.

Expenzing · 2025

Catching mismatches automatically instead of by hand

S
Three-way matching exceptions (PO vs. receipt vs. invoice) were reviewed manually each week.
T
Reduce the manual load without missing real discrepancies.
A
Implemented automated exception-flagging rules inside the matching workflow so only true variances reached a reviewer.
R
35% less reconciliation effort, an estimated ~$250K a year saved.
