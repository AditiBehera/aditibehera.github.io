Expenzing · Business Analyst · 2025

Finding the hidden batch in a 7-layer approval chain

Problem
Payables were slow and nobody could say where time was lost.
Did
Mapped every approval layer, measured hours per transaction, found a 10-item batch step holding invoices.
Result
Cycle time down 14.6%; fix shipped with IT within days.

At Walmart: the same root-cause habit applies to marketplace escalations that sit in queues, or inbound loads that wait on a planning step.

Expenzing · Automation · 2025

Letting the system flag exceptions so people don't have to

Problem
Analysts checked invoice, PO, and receipt mismatches by hand.
Did
Built automated exception-flagging into three-way matching, so only true mismatches reached a person.
Result
35% less manual effort; ~$250K a year saved.

At Walmart: Seller Marketplace is building AI tools to speed up case handling. This is the same idea at smaller scale.
