Selected work

Fixing an alert that missed a spike

Problem
One retailer's cancellations jumped from 3.2% to 5.7%. The standard 3-sigma alert caught only the first week, then stopped firing, and it also raised a false alarm on a steady retailer.
What I did
Kept known incidents out of the baseline and required a move of at least 0.5 points. Then I split cancellations by reason and drilled into stores.
Result
The new alert caught 4 of 4 weeks with no false alarms. I traced the spike to 6 stores whose hours changed, sized it at about 111 lost orders a week, and recommended three fixes.

Built on a synthetic dataset I generated: 240,000 orders across six retailers.

Unblocking invoice approvals

Problem
Enterprise clients' invoices kept stalling in approval at Expenzing, a Source-to-Pay SaaS company.
What I did
Traced the workflow end to end and found that invoices moved in batches of 10, and every batch had to clear seven approval layers in order.
Result
I took the evidence to IT, and they shipped a workaround.
